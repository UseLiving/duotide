# Duotide

The dual-market launchpad. **One token, born on two markets at once.** Their price gap is yours to close.

**Live site:** https://useliving.github.io/duotide/  
**X:** https://x.com/UseDuotide

## What you can do
- Dive through the water: every bubble is a token, sorted by depth (live, opening, registered)
- Click a bubble to read its live spread, click again to open it
- Click empty water to create a token: ticker, name, opening buys, bridge stock
- Trade on either market, or **Earn the gap** in one click (instantly, or by the bridge)
- Browse the market table, how it works, tokenomics and live stats
- Play wallet with $1,000 and a faucet

Single self-contained `index.html`. Progress is saved in your browser.

> Simulation only. Surface and Deep are pretend markets. No real money, wallets or chains.
